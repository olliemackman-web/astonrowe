FAQ

Questions we get asked.

I already have a website. Why would I need a new one?

If it isn't regularly bringing you enquiries, it isn't doing its job. Our free audit shows exactly where your current site is losing leads. If it only needs fixing rather than rebuilding, we'll tell you.

How much does a website cost?

Less than you might think. We keep our websites affordable for trade and construction businesses, and you'll get a clear quote for your site before any work starts. Tell us what you need and we'll send you a price.

Do I need to write the content myself?

No. We write all the copy for you after a short call about your business, your services and the work you want more of. You just check it over.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site is built for local search from day one: proper page structure, service and area pages, fast load times and a Google Business Profile set up to work with it.

What if I need changes after it goes live?

Just send us a message. We handle hosting, updates and changes, so you never have to log into anything or worry about it breaking.

I don't have many good photos of my work. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We can work with phone photos, help pick your best shots, and tell you what to capture on your next few jobs so your gallery keeps getting stronger.
